From the publisher:

In the blue hour before dawn, a small coastal town wakes to news that will reshape everything its residents believed about each other. Over the course of a single day — from the first ferry to the last — four lives quietly intersect:

A lighthouse keeper who has spent thirty years keeping a secret from the town he loves. A young soldier returning to a home that no longer feels like his. A mother navigating the slow unraveling of a marriage she thought was safe. And a stranger who arrives with a small leather bag and the weight of something long unsaid.

E. M. Hale writes with extraordinary restraint, letting silence do as much work as dialogue. The result is a novel that feels less written than remembered — a book about the small hours we keep, the people we think we know, and the moments that change everything.
